# Metric contract

The date axis and slicer use **purchase date** throughout the report. All monetary values are BRL. This is a historical marketplace dataset and contains no complete cost, commission, refund-ledger or accounting-recognition model.

| Metric | Definition | Population / denominator |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Placed orders | Count of unique orders | Every status, including canceled/unavailable/open orders |
| Delivered orders | Count of orders with `order_status = delivered` | One order, independent of items/payments/reviews |
| Delivered GMV | Sum of item `price_cents` in delivered orders / 100 | Excludes freight, canceled orders and non-delivered orders |
| Delivered freight | Sum of item `freight_value_cents` in delivered orders / 100 | Same status population as GMV |
| Average ticket | Delivered GMV / delivered order count | Freight excluded; blank if denominator is zero |
| Cancellation rate | Canceled order count / all placed orders | `unavailable` is a distinct status, not silently reclassified as canceled |
| Recorded payments | Sum of `payment_value_cents` / 100 | All payment rows, all order statuses |
| Payment difference | Recorded payment cents minus item price plus freight cents | At order grain, all statuses |
| Orders with a payment difference | Orders where `ABS(payment_difference_cents) > 1` | One-cent tolerance is explicit; no source values are changed |
| Observed deliveries | Delivered status + nonnull actual timestamp + nonnegative duration | Excludes ongoing, canceled and invalid/missing delivery observations |
| Late deliveries | Actual delivery calendar date > estimated calendar date | Only observed valid deliveries |
| Late-delivery rate | Late deliveries / observed deliveries | Missing timestamps are excluded, not treated as on time |
| Average delivery days | Mean of elapsed timestamp seconds / 86,400 | Observed valid deliveries; includes fractional calendar days |
| Average rating | Mean latest `review_score` per delivered order with a rating | Missing reviews are excluded, not scored as zero |
| Rating coverage | Delivered orders with a latest rating / delivered orders | Coverage is separate from the average score |
| Unique delivered customers | Distinct `customer_unique_id` with a delivered order | Historical source window only |
| Repeat customers | Customers with >1 delivered order | Historical source window only, not lifetime retention |
| RFM recency | Days since last delivered purchase to a fixed reference date | Day after the latest purchase in the complete input, not today's date |
| RFM frequency | Delivered order count per `customer_unique_id` | Not item count or payment count |
| RFM monetary | Delivered merchandise cents per `customer_unique_id` | No freight, no predictive lifetime value |

## Interpretation boundaries

GMV is not the marketplace's revenue. No profit, margin, commission, acquisition cost, refund-adjusted net revenue or predicted CLV is inferred. Recorded payments are source records; they are not proof of bank settlement. Differences can involve canceled/no-item orders, source inconsistencies and payment details that the dataset does not fully explain.

Category and seller analyses use item-level merchandise value. When a product/seller filter is active, order measures count relevant distinct orders through DAX `TREATAS`; the GMV numerator contains only selected items. Thus a category ticket describes selected category merchandise per relevant order, not the entire mixed-category basket.

The dataset ends in October 2018 and its boundary months are sparse. Orders near the observation cutoff may be incomplete. Do not compare those months as fully closed periods or describe the ending curve as a business failure. Month-over-month DAX measures are supplied as arithmetic tools; period completeness remains an analytical judgement.

Delivery and ratings are observational. A lower rating among late deliveries is an association, not proof that delay alone caused dissatisfaction. Product mix, geography, seasonality, selection and unobserved factors can affect the relationship. Rating coverage is reported to make selection visible.

## Reference implementation

- Python: `src/retail_analytics/transform.py` and `pipeline.py`.
- SQL: `sql/marts/` and `sql/analysis/`.
- DAX: `powerbi/measures.dax`, generated by `scripts/build_powerbi.py`.
- Verified baseline: `docs/evidence/summary.json` and `analysis.json`.

The pipeline independently reconciles item sums, freight, payments and GMV across orders, months, categories, states and customers. It does not require the source's payment amount to equal items plus freight, because that would hide a legitimate reconciliation question.
